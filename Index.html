CG Files Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how CG Files handles device storage, permissions, advertising, third-party services and privacy.

Important: CG Files is designed as an offline-first file-management application. Core file-management operations are intended to operate locally on your Android device. Third-party services, such as advertising, may process information separately according to their own policies.

1. Our Privacy Approach

Chirag Tech Studios ("we", "us", or "our") operates the CG Files mobile application ("CG Files", "the App").

CG Files is designed primarily as a local, offline-first file-management application. The core file-management functionality is designed to operate on the user's device.

CG Files does not require users to create an account or log in to use its core file-management features.

2. Files and Device Storage

CG Files requires access to device storage so that users can browse and manage their own files.

Depending on the Android version and storage mechanism used by the App, CG Files may access files and folders that the user has explicitly authorized the App to access.

This access may be required for:

  • Browsing files and folders
  • Viewing images and videos
  • Managing audio files
  • Managing documents
  • Managing downloads
  • Managing APK files
  • Searching files
  • Copying and moving files
  • Renaming files
  • Deleting supported files
  • Viewing file information
  • Storage analysis

CG Files does not intentionally upload the contents of your personal files to Chirag Tech Studios servers for ordinary file-management operations.

3. Permissions

CG Files may request storage access or other Android-supported file-access mechanisms required for its file-management functionality.

These permissions or access mechanisms may be used for:

  • Reading files
  • Displaying files
  • Creating folders
  • Modifying files
  • Copying files
  • Moving files
  • Renaming files
  • Deleting files
  • Analyzing storage

CG Files does not request access to contacts, microphone, camera, or location unless a future feature specifically requires such permission and users are appropriately informed.

4. Advertising — Google AdMob

CG Files may display advertisements using Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK.

Because third-party advertising services may process certain device and advertising information, advertising-related data handling is different from the local file-management functionality of CG Files.

Depending on the advertising configuration, device settings, applicable laws, consent choices, and advertising technology used, Google and its advertising partners may process information such as:

  • Advertising identifiers
  • Device and app information
  • IP address or related network information
  • Approximate location derived from network information
  • Ad interaction information
  • Diagnostic and performance information
  • Information used for advertising measurement
  • Information used for personalized or non-personalized advertising

Advertising-related processing is subject to Google's applicable privacy and advertising policies.

Chirag Tech Studios does not control all data processing performed by Google or other third-party advertising providers.

5. Personalized and Non-Personalized Advertising

Depending on the user's region, consent choices, device settings and the application's advertising configuration, advertisements may be personalized or non-personalized.

Where legally required, appropriate consent mechanisms may be presented to users.

6. Third-Party Services

CG Files may use third-party services to provide advertising, diagnostics, analytics, or other application functionality.

The primary advertising service currently intended for CG Files is Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK.

Third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

If additional third-party services are included in a released version, applicable privacy disclosures will be updated where necessary.

7. Analytics and Diagnostics

CG Files may use technical diagnostics, crash reporting, analytics, or similar services in future versions to improve application stability and performance.

If such services are included in a released version, the applicable data collection and processing will be reflected in the application's privacy disclosures and Google Play Data Safety information.

CG Files does not intentionally use analytics to collect the contents of users' personal files.

8. Vault and Security Features

CG Files may provide a local Vault feature for protecting selected files.

  • Vault content is intended to remain on the user's device.
  • Sensitive authentication information should not be stored as plain text.
  • Android security facilities may be used for sensitive data.
  • Encryption may be used for supported protected content.
  • Users are responsible for remembering their Vault credentials.

No security system can guarantee absolute protection against every possible threat. Users should maintain backups of important files.

9. Recycle Bin

If the Recycle Bin feature is enabled, supported deleted files may be moved to an application-managed recycle area instead of being immediately permanently deleted.

Users may be able to restore or permanently delete supported files. Exact behavior may vary depending on Android storage restrictions and file location.

10. File Sharing

When you choose to share a file using CG Files, Android's system sharing mechanism or another appropriate Android mechanism may be used.

File sharing occurs as a result of an action initiated by the user. Users should verify the destination application or recipient before sharing sensitive files.

11. Third-Party Applications

CG Files may open files using applications installed on your Android device.

CG Files does not control the privacy practices of third-party applications. Users should review the privacy policies of those applications before sharing sensitive information with them.

12. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the application.

Security measures may include:

  • Android platform security mechanisms
  • Secure application storage
  • Appropriate access controls
  • Secure file-sharing mechanisms
  • Encryption for supported sensitive Vault data
  • Secure handling of application credentials

However, no electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

13. Internet Connectivity

Core file-management functionality is designed to work without an internet connection where Android permissions and the relevant feature permit.

Internet connectivity may be required for third-party services such as advertising, application updates, or optional online functionality.

14. Data Retention and Deletion

CG Files does not require an online user account containing your personal files.

Files managed by CG Files normally remain on your device under your control.

Users can remove locally stored application data through Android settings or uninstall the application.

Advertising-related information processed by third-party providers may be subject to those providers' own retention and deletion practices.

15. Children's Privacy

CG Files is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

We do not intentionally request personal information from children through an account-registration system.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us in a manner that violates applicable law, please contact us using the information below.

16. Data Sharing

Chirag Tech Studios does not sell users' personal information.

CG Files does not intentionally share users' personal file contents with Chirag Tech Studios servers for ordinary file-management operations.

Information may be processed by third-party services necessary for application functionality, such as advertising providers, according to their applicable policies and the application's configuration.

Information may also be disclosed where required to comply with applicable law, legal process, court orders, or legitimate government requests.

17. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the application, its features, third-party services, advertising configuration, or applicable legal requirements change.

When significant changes are made, the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy will be updated.

20. Final Statement

CG Files is designed to give users convenient control over their files while keeping ordinary file-management operations primarily on the user's device.

We respect user privacy and aim to process only information necessary for application functionality, advertising, security, diagnostics, and improvement.
